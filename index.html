Warm-in Technique Situations Live go
Pad: hold your thumb in the green band to stay in stance. Drag down fast for a level change. Slide left or right to move your feet. Let go and you stand up.
Camera play: prop the device sideways at hip height about 6 ft away (on an iPad, Center Stage off in Control Center). It calibrates you first: three holds, then shuffle to your left until it beeps or you reach your wall, pause, then all the way to your right and pause. With the back camera the screen faces away, so it all runs by sound.
Block: a down block, hands to his head and shoulders, hips back, feet planted. It stops a shot from range for little effort; a deep shot runs through it and needs the sprawl.
Sprawl, shot, snap: your big moves. Each round, the coach calls them and the drill partner shows the look.
Live go reads:
red dip = shot coming, sprawl amber arms = tie-up, snap green reach = opening, shoot orange drive = he's pushing you out, circle off
After a stuffed shot or a snap, slide left or right to go behind. Hips high for too long and he attacks more.
Your floor: the strip at the bottom of the mat is your training space, wall to wall. The orange ends are the edge of the mat. Step out in the live go and he scores 1, so circle off the edge. Near center you move freely; past halfway his circling drifts you back home, and at the wall he cuts you off.
Skip: the skip button ends the current round, keeps its data, and starts the next round with no reset.
